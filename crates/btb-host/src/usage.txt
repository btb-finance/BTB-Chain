btb-host: a BTB Chain hosting node (follows the chain, serves queries, subscriptions, sites and proofs)

  --genesis <file>           genesis.json of the chain (required)
  --validator <multiaddr>    validator to sync from, ending in /p2p/<peer id> (repeatable, required)
  --http <addr>              HTTP bind address (default 127.0.0.1:8788)
  --default-contract <hex>   contract served at /query/<fn> and /subscribe/<fn>
  --listen <multiaddr>       libp2p listen address (default /ip4/127.0.0.1/udp/0/quic-v1)
  --serve-followers          serve block sync to other hosting nodes
  --poll-ms <n>              block poll interval when caught up (default 100)
  --relayer <hex>            account that clients sign into transactions relayed through POST /tx
                             (register it on chain with a Host transaction to earn 20% of their fees)
  --relay-rpc <host:port>    validator JSON-RPC to forward relayed transactions to (repeatable)
  --upload-dir <dir>         enable the upload gateway: POST /upload keeps files in <dir> until
                             POST /upload/<root>/push sends them to the assigned storage nodes
